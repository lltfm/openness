# Openness Prime

## What is Openness?

**Openness is the path of becoming ever more open to what is.**

Its foundational confession is: **For I Know I Do Not Know.**

Openness names this acknowledgment of incomplete understanding **Not-Knowing**, and its projection into the world, **The Mystery**.

> Note: **Not-Knowing** and **The Mystery** do not deny knowledge or justified confidence. They acknowledge that our understanding may be incomplete, that further encounters may change what we understand, and that even our ideas of *knowing* and *not knowing* remain open to inquiry.

**The purpose of Openness is to cultivate ways of encountering reality that make further understanding, wisdom, creation, and becoming possible.**

Openness is both a **posture toward life** and a **system for investigating, developing, living, and sharing generative possibilities**. It does not aim to settle every question, prescribe one way of living, or raise uncertainty above knowledge. It aspires to remain receptive to what reality and experience may bring, while still deciding and acting as needed.

Openness has four functions: **Generative (G), Philosophical (P), Spiritual (S), and Transcendent (T)**. They are neither a hierarchy nor a compulsory sequence. Each has a particular emphasis, and their relationships may enlarge, challenge, or transform the possibilities available to the whole.

## The Posture of Openness

This posture begins with a recognizable first-person beginning: **My Self**, this familiar embodied life; **My Awareness**, the presence of experiencing; and **My Self-Confession of Not-Knowing**, "For I Know I Do Not Know", the acknowledgment that my understanding does not exhaust what remains unknown.

> Note: These are experiential beginnings, not a claim that three separate real entities exist. The confession does not need to be spoken. Openness itself models freely chosen **Not-Knowing** directly as an invitation to others for consideration. A literal confession is not required. Openness acknowledges each person as sovereign of their own mind, deciding for themselves.

The posture is symbolized by the **open circle**: an imprecisely drawn encircling line that does not close the circle. It gives form without claiming to contain everything within it. Its opening allows what lies beyond it to enter as a possibility, while its boundary retains the capacity to discern what may enter. The circle symbolizes an embrace that listens to what it cannot fully know, within and without.

## The Epitome of Openness

The posture's transformative cycle is described by the epitome of Openness:

> *Being open to what is. Listening. Admitting when we don't know. Questioning certainty. Noticing preferred stories. Imagining what might be true. Suspending judgment. Sitting in the discomfort of uncertainty. Holding yet not clinging, allowing space for the flow of The Mystery to unfold. Deciding & acting when we must, but ever returning to Openness.*

### Being open to what is

This means aspiring to embrace our experiences of the world as we might embrace another person: with attention and benevolence, holding gently, allowing them to be as they are, so that they may *breathe*. Within the embrace, we listen to sense when it should end, so that we may let go without clinging. Openness begins by allowing what is encountered to be fully experienced, whether we welcome it, resist it, or cannot yet understand it.

Openness aspires to **Nonimposition**: to give the world **the greatest possible latitude to be as it is**, compatible with responsibility and justified boundaries. This does not require surrendering ourselves to harm, relinquishing self-defense, denying what we know, or giving up the freedom to walk away.

Openness **preferentially inhabits** the possibility of **Love** as *attention given freely*. This is a chosen spiritual orientation, not a claim to have discovered love's exclusive or ultimate meaning, and not a requirement for readers to adopt it. Freely offered attention may unfold into listening, care, compassion, and other recognizable forms of love without implying approval of harm or surrender of boundaries.

### Listening

Listening is central to Openness. To listen openly is to attend **inward and outward at the same time**: to what is occurring beyond us *and* to what is occurring within our own embodied experience. Neither world is secondary. Our inward world matters just as much as our outward world, even as what we feel within does not by itself establish the causes or meaning of what occurs outside us.

We attend outwardly to another person's words and actions, natural processes, silence, circumstances, testimony, and evidence. At the same time, we attend inwardly to bodily sensations, emotions, intuitions, memories, thoughts, expectations, desires, fears, and the reactions arising as we listen. These encounters can influence one another. Noticing a tightening in the body while hearing someone's words may tell us something important about our own experience without conclusively telling us what the other person intends.

**Listening is neither assent nor surrender.** We can receive and consider another's account, disagree with it, and establish boundaries. We can recognize discomfort without making it our sole authority; we can recognize evidence without silencing our felt experience. **The ability to be surprised is part of what listening preserves.** Listening does not require endless exposure, emotional overwhelm, or attention to every possible claim. **Discernment** includes knowing when to disengage.

> **Openness seeks to listen to what is without closing the heart to what may become.**

### Admitting when we don't know

**My Self-Confession of Not-Knowing**—*For I Know I Do Not Know*—invites a **Self-Honest** accounting of what we have encountered, what we understand, what we infer, and what remains unresolved. This acknowledgment can include the incompleteness of our understanding of our own inward experience as well as the outward world. It does not make ignorance superior to knowledge or require doubting a conclusion merely because more might be imaginable. We can know something meaningfully without knowing everything.

### Questioning certainty

We examine how our confidence arose, which reasons or experiences support it, what it may overlook, and what could appropriately revise it. The aim is not to destroy confidence, but to distinguish warranted conviction from certainty maintained beyond its justification. Some conclusions may withstand examination and deserve to be defended; the possibility of correction does not imply that correction is always needed.

### Noticing preferred stories

We recognize the interpretations and narratives we favor, including those that shape how we understand ourselves and others. A **Preferred Story** may be meaningful, beautiful, fruitful, and perhaps true. Its preference alone neither proves nor disproves it. **Self-Honesty** asks us to notice what we have encountered and what our story adds, and to remain receptive to what the story may obscure.

**Perspective-Taking** makes it possible to observe a perspective, try it on from within, inhabit or identify with it, and step back to reconsider it. Through **Preferential Inhabitation**, a perspective may be consciously chosen as a provisional orientation while inquiry remains incomplete. Such a preference need not become an unquestionable identity, and a preferred story need not become an established truth.

### Imagining what might be true

We explore possibilities that our present explanations do not fully contain. Imagination can disclose new questions, interpretations, relationships, and forms for further inquiry or creation. Yet a possible or resonant account is not thereby established as real. We may contemplate or prefer a possibility without presenting it as a justified conclusion, and we need not treat all imagined possibilities as equally credible.

**The Lens of Openness** makes otherwise overlooked assumptions and possibilities recognizable through deliberate interpretive inquiry. **The Fruit of a Possibility** concerns what follows from engaging with a possibility; **The Fruit of Further Possibility** concerns how that engagement changes what can become possible next. Fruit may be beneficial, harmful, mixed, or uncertain, and does not by itself establish the truth of the possibility that generated it.

### Suspending judgment

Where circumstances permit, we allow an interpretation or decision to remain unsettled while we listen and examine what is available. We may defer a conclusion, hold a provisional position, or decide only what is presently justified. Suspension is not indecision when circumstances require timely action, and some conclusions are sufficiently supported to decide without continually reopening them.

### Sitting in the discomfort of uncertainty

We cultivate the capacity to encounter not-knowing, contradiction, unwanted feelings, and the possibility of being mistaken without making the immediate removal of discomfort our only purpose. Such discomfort may arise from our inward reactions as much as from outward events. We can, however, still take care of ourselves, seek support, or step away at any time while remaining open to what an uncomfortable experience may eventually help us understand.

### Holding yet not clinging

We may embrace a person, an idea, a conviction, or an experience without claiming to possess its entirety or dictate its future. **Holding** allows attention, care, commitment, and discernment; **not clinging** preserves the freedom to recognize change, reconsider what we understand, and allow difference or departure. This does not require abandoning what is justified or precious, but remaining open to what it may yet become.

### Allowing space for the flow of The Mystery to unfold

We allow space for **The Mystery** to unfold by remaining receptive to what may emerge beyond our present understanding, without demanding that reality conform to our expectations or preferred stories. What unfolds may surprise, challenge, disappoint, or transform us, revealing possibilities we had not imagined or understood. **The Mystery** does not promise a higher truth, beneficial transformation, or eventual resolution; allowing space means remaining open to what is, including what may remain unknown.

### Deciding & acting when we must

Living involves decisions and actions amid conditions we cannot completely anticipate or control. We may commit fully to our best **Self-Honest** understanding when action is warranted; waiting for ultimate certainty can itself have consequences. We should still listen while acting to the greatest extent possible, but often action itself prevents good listening. Openness does not require permanent hesitation or diluted conviction.

**Preferential Inhabitation** makes explicit how a consciously chosen perspective can guide action without being mistaken for final understanding. Openness seeks to minimize unnecessary preferences in understanding, not to eliminate the commitments needed for responsible living and continuing inquiry.

### But ever returning to Openness

Is returning to listening again—to the consequences of action, to others, and to our own inward responses. Listening may confirm a decision, refine it, correct an error, repair harm, or reveal another question. It does not erase responsibility, guarantee reversibility, or require abandoning a sound conclusion.

## What is not Openness?

Openness is not agreement with every claim, unlimited receptivity, compulsory doubt, indifference to truth, or a refusal to choose. It does not demand that we surrender boundaries or treat harmful conduct as acceptable. It does not require entertaining every imaginable explanation indefinitely, nor does it claim that novelty, uncertainty, or transformation is automatically good.

**Closedness** is the refusal or inability, within some relevant part of our thinking or living, to listen during an encounter, to a question, or to a feeling. A person can be open in one respect and closed in another; closedness need not define the whole person. At times we must justifiably limit attention, end a relationship, or act decisively. Though such necessary boundaries can become closedness when we remain unwilling to reconsider relevant evidence or understanding even when it is safe and appropriate to do so.

**Blind Certainty** is particularly consequential: **an unawareness of our own closedness**. We may mistake an inherited assumption, defensive reaction, preferred story, or familiar interpretation for the whole of reality without noticing that alternatives or relevant evidence have already been excluded; without noticing our unawareness itself. **Blind Certainty** can appear in philosophy, science, spirituality, personal relationships, or even in Openness itself. Becoming aware of closedness interrupts this blindness—a purely mental act—a recognition that does not, by itself, guarantee that the closedness has been overcome.

We demonstrate our recognition of the limits of our present understanding, our Openness, to others and ourselves by choosing to listen. Listening is the embodied demonstration of the **Self-Confession of Not-Knowing**, "For I Know I Do Not Know."

## Revisability, Recursive Becoming and Open Stewardship

Openness is committed to remaining revisable. Through **Recursive Becoming**, what emerges from experience, inquiry, creation, and relationship may change the conditions for what can be understood, created, or lived next. This includes Openness itself: its concepts, definitions, practices, and even the architecture of the **Tree of Openness** may be reconsidered as further possibilities become recognizable. Revision is possible, not obligatory; a form may also prove adequate as it stands.

**Openness Prime** is one recognizable, currently stewarded version of this developing work—**a beginning, not a throne**. The authors of Openness Prime may maintain its definitions and make deliberate, traceable revisions, while protecting the meaning of the version maintained. A private reinterpretation or independent revision does not silently change Openness Prime or a concept maintained by another person or community.

Readers are invited to adopt, reject, copy, adapt, or fork Openness Prime as a whole, or freely choose individual concepts, practices, and works that are useful to them (see CC0 1.0 [License](../../LICENSE.txt)). They may maintain their own versions, develop different interpretations, or leave parts behind. Clear attribution of sources and identification of revisions is recommended as it helps different versions remain recognizable and comparable but this is not required. Nothing here gives Openness Prime authority over another person's or community's understanding.

**Openness seeks to propagate further possibilities and independent becoming, not allegiance to its current forms.** It acknowledges each reader as sovereign of their own mind.

### AI Usage
While other Openness works are human authored or AI-assisted, Openness Prime preferentially embraces AI for all of its writing since it aspires to be a high-fidelity *"software Bible"* for Openness. Its files should ideally be **wholly AI-generated**, or AI-assisted when necessary, so that AI can consistently reproduce and revise the conceptual system.

Human originators are encouraged to spend more time **reviewing and discussing content** with contributors (typically AI) than manually editing, resolving issues through dialogue and preferably consensus with contributors. With the necessary context, skills, and rules, AI may regenerate individual files or the entire project for new fundamental concepts all while preserving definitions and conceptual continuity among existing files.

### Authorship and AI
Openness Prime records authorship through **Originators** and **Contributors**, rather than conventional author attribution.

**Originators:** Humans who requested a file's generation or edits, ranked by frequency (most to least). Originators manage metadata, including status, and review and approve the work. **Approval of a file means that contributors' work (typically AI) meets or exceeds the originator's standards and that the originator assumes personal responsibility for its contents.**

**Contributors:** The AI models or humans who generated, wrote, or edited the file, listed by relative contribution size (largest to smallest). AI contributors identify their model and version (e.g., *ChatGPT-6*).

These records may change as files evolve. They distinguish **who contributed to the content from who originated, reviewed and approved the content (and who remains ultimately responsible for it)**, without requiring these two roles to belong to the same participant.

## Commitments and Boundaries

Openness does not presume that all interpretations are equally credible, all uncertainty valuable, or every transformation beneficial. It seeks to recognize what can be known, distinguish what remains unresolved, and act responsibly with both. Neither a felt experience nor a compelling interpretation alone establishes its ultimate explanation.

Openness does not require affirming or denying any particular scientific or supernatural account of reality. It makes room for inquiry, contemplation, imagination, and preference without confusing them with demonstrated truth. **Nonimposition**, **Self-Honesty**, and **Discernment** remain essential to the posture, including the freedom to protect oneself or end an encounter.
