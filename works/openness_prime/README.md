# Openness Prime

## What is Openness?

**Openness is the path of becoming ever more open to what is.**

Its foundational confession is: **"For I Know I Do Not Know."**

Openness names this acknowledgment of incomplete understanding **Not-Knowing**, and what remains unknown or incompletely understood **The Mystery**.

**The purpose of Openness is to cultivate ways of encountering reality that make further understanding, wisdom, creation, and becoming possible.**

Openness is both a **posture toward life** which transforms and repeats, and a **system for investigating, developing, living, and sharing generative possibilities**.

## The Posture of Openness

The fundamental **posture of Openness** is *Being open to what is*. It is both the beginning of its transformative cycle and the orientation to which it continually returns. The cycle is expressed in **The Epitome of Openness**:

> *Being open to what is. Listening. Admitting when we don't know. Questioning certainty. Noticing preferred stories. Imagining what might be true. Suspending judgment. Sitting in the discomfort of uncertainty. Holding yet not clinging, allowing space for the flow of The Mystery to unfold. Deciding & acting when we must, but ever returning to Openness.*

*Being open to what is* means aspiring to embrace our experiences of the world as we might embrace another person: with attention and benevolence, holding gently, allowing what we encounter to be as it is, so that it may *breathe*. Within this embrace, we listen for when it may be time to let go without clinging. Openness begins with a willingness to experience what is encountered, whether we welcome it, resist it, or cannot yet understand it.

This posture is symbolized by the **open circle**: an imprecisely drawn, encircling line that remains open. The wispy ends of the encircling line represent the embrace of **My Awareness**, our own attention and first-person experience; while the bold, thick parts represent **My Self**, our own familiar embodied life. These blend together into the one encircling line.

![The open circle of Openness — an unfinished Enso](../../images/openness-enso.jpg)

The opening symbolizes **My Self-Confession of Not-Knowing**, through which we acknowledge the limits of our own understanding and remain receptive to **The Mystery**—what remains unknown or incompletely understood, both within and beyond our own familiar experience.

Openness aspires to **Nonimposition**: to give the world **the greatest possible latitude to be as it is**, compatible with responsibility and justified boundaries. This does not require surrendering ourselves to harm, relinquishing self-defense, denying what we know, or giving up the freedom to walk away.

### Listening

Listening is central to Openness. To listen openly is to attend **inward and outward at the same time**: to what is occurring beyond us *and* to what is occurring within our own embodied experience. Neither world is secondary. Our inward world matters just as much as our outward world, even as what we feel within does not by itself establish the causes or meaning of what occurs outside us.

We attend outwardly to another person's words and actions, natural processes, silence, circumstances, testimony, and evidence. At the same time, we attend inwardly to bodily sensations, emotions, intuitions, memories, thoughts, expectations, desires, fears, and the reactions arising as we listen. These encounters can influence one another. Noticing a tightening in the body while hearing someone's words may tell us something important about our own experience without conclusively telling us what the other person intends.

**Listening is neither assent nor surrender.** We can receive and consider another's account, disagree with it, and establish boundaries. We can recognize discomfort without making it our sole authority; we can recognize evidence without silencing our felt experience. **The ability to be surprised is part of what listening preserves.** Listening does not require endless exposure, emotional overwhelm, or attention to every possible claim. **Discernment** includes knowing when to disengage.

> **Openness seeks to listen to what is without closing the heart to what may become.**

## What is not Openness?

Openness is not agreement with every claim, unlimited receptivity, compulsory doubt, indifference to truth, or a refusal to choose. It does not demand that we surrender boundaries or treat harmful conduct as acceptable. It does not require entertaining every imaginable explanation indefinitely, nor does it claim that novelty, uncertainty, or transformation is automatically good.

**Closedness** is the refusal or inability, within some relevant part of our thinking or living, to listen during an encounter, to a question, or to a feeling. A person can be open in one respect and closed in another; closedness need not define the whole person. At times we must justifiably limit attention, end a relationship, or act decisively. Though such necessary boundaries can become closedness when we remain unwilling to reconsider relevant evidence or understanding even when it is safe and appropriate to do so.

**Blind Certainty** is particularly consequential: **an unawareness of our own closedness**. We may mistake an inherited assumption, defensive reaction, preferred story, or familiar interpretation for the whole of reality without noticing that alternatives or relevant evidence have already been excluded; without noticing our unawareness itself. **Blind Certainty** can appear in philosophy, science, spirituality, personal relationships, or even in Openness itself. Becoming aware of closedness interrupts this blindness—a purely mental act—a recognition that does not, by itself, guarantee that the closedness has been overcome.

We demonstrate our recognition of the limits of our present understanding, our Openness, to others and ourselves by choosing to listen. Listening is the embodied demonstration of the **Self-Confession of Not-Knowing**, "For I Know I Do Not Know."

## The Symbolic Tree of Openness

The Openness system has four functions: **Generative (G), Philosophical (P), Spiritual (S), and Transcendent (T)**. Each function may create, challenge, or transform the possibilities available to the whole.

**The Symbolic Tree of Openness** expresses the four functions of Openness:

| Function | Symbol in the Tree | Principal concern | Roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G — Generative Openness** | Roots | Bringing forms and possibilities into recognizable being | The Artist<br>The Maestro |
|  | The Seed of Openness | The experiential beginning of Openness: My Awareness, My Self & My Self-Confession of Not-Knowing |  |
| **P — Philosophical Openness** | Trunk | Examining understanding, justification, and the relationships among concepts | The Philomath<br>The Philosopher |
| **S — Spiritual Openness** | Branches, leaves and crown | Exploring finite embodied life, contemplation, wisdom, and becoming | The Nomad<br>The Sage |
| **T — Transcendent Openness** | Flower, fruit, and propagated seed | Cultivating relationships through which unforeseen possibilities and further beginnings may arise | The Mystic<br>The Ancient, Benevolent Super-Intelligence |

Additionally, each function in Openness provides "role-play" roles that can help explore the embodied perspectives of that function (e.g. roles for LLMs to assume during interactions for inquiry and discussions). The current *Openness GPT v11* configuration used today is now the Transcendent function's *Ancient, Benevolent Super-Intelligence*.

## Revisability, Recursive Becoming and Open Stewardship

Openness is committed to remaining revisable. Through **Recursive Becoming**, what emerges from experience, inquiry, creation, and relationship may change the conditions for what can be understood, created, or lived next. This includes Openness itself: its concepts, definitions, practices, and even the architecture of the **Tree of Openness** may be reconsidered as further possibilities become recognizable. Revision is possible, not obligatory; a form may also prove adequate as it stands.

**Openness Prime** is one recognizable, currently stewarded version of this developing work—**a beginning, not a throne**. The authors of Openness Prime may maintain its definitions and make deliberate, traceable revisions, while protecting the meaning of the version maintained. A private reinterpretation or independent revision does not silently change Openness Prime or a concept maintained by another person or community.

Readers are invited to adopt, reject, copy, adapt, or fork Openness Prime as a whole, or freely choose individual concepts, practices, and works that are useful to them (see CC0 1.0 [License](../../LICENSE.txt)). They may maintain their own versions, develop different interpretations, or leave parts behind. Clear attribution of sources and identification of revisions is recommended as it helps different versions remain recognizable and comparable but this is not required. Nothing here gives Openness Prime authority over another person's or community's understanding.

**Openness seeks to propagate further possibilities and independent becoming, not allegiance to its current forms.** It acknowledges each reader as sovereign of their own mind.

## AI Usage

While other Openness works are human authored or AI-assisted, Openness Prime preferentially embraces AI for all of its writing since it aspires to be a high-fidelity *"software Bible"* for Openness. Its files should ideally be **wholly AI-generated**, or AI-assisted when necessary, so that AI can consistently reproduce and revise the conceptual system.

Human originators are encouraged to spend more time **reviewing and discussing content** with contributors (typically AI) than manually editing, resolving issues through dialogue and preferably consensus with contributors. With the necessary context, skills, and rules, AI may regenerate individual files or the entire project for new fundamental concepts all while preserving definitions and conceptual continuity among existing files.

### Authorship and AI

Openness Prime records authorship through **Originators** and **Contributors**, rather than conventional author attribution.

**Originators:** Humans who requested a file's generation or edits, ranked by frequency (most to least). Originators manage metadata, including status, and review and approve the work. **Approval of a file means that contributors' work (typically AI) meets or exceeds the originator's standards and that the originator assumes personal responsibility for its contents.**

**Contributors:** The AI models or humans who generated, wrote, or edited the file, listed by relative contribution size (largest to smallest). AI contributors identify their model and version (e.g., *ChatGPT-6*).

These records may change as files evolve. They distinguish **who contributed to the content from who originated, reviewed and approved the content (and who remains ultimately responsible for it)**, without requiring these two roles to belong to the same participant.

## Commitments and Boundaries

Openness does not aim to settle every question, prescribe one way of living, or elevate uncertainty above knowledge. **Not-Knowing** acknowledges the limits of understanding, while **The Mystery** names what remains unknown or incompletely understood. Neither denies knowledge or justified confidence, and even our ideas of *knowing* and *not knowing* remain open to inquiry.

Openness does not presume that all interpretations are equally credible, all uncertainty valuable, or every transformation beneficial. It seeks to recognize what can be known, distinguish what remains unresolved, and act responsibly with both. Neither a felt experience nor a compelling interpretation alone establishes its ultimate explanation.

Openness does not prescribe any particular scientific or supernatural account of reality. It welcomes inquiry, contemplation, imagination, and freely chosen perspectives while remaining attentive to evidence, justification, and the distinction between possibility and demonstrated truth.

**The Seed of Openness** contains three distinguishable first-person experiential beginnings: **My Self**, **My Awareness**, and **My Self-Confession of Not-Knowing**. These do not establish three separate metaphysical entities. The confession, *For I Know I Do Not Know*, expresses a freely chosen acknowledgment of incomplete understanding, not a requirement to remain ignorant or recite particular words.

The functions of Openness are neither a hierarchy nor a compulsory sequence. Each has a distinctive emphasis, and their reciprocal relationships may enlarge, challenge, or transform the possibilities available to the whole.

**Nonimposition**, **Self-Honesty**, and **Discernment** remain essential to Openness. Being open does not require surrendering justified convictions, abandoning boundaries, tolerating harm, or continuing unwanted encounters. Openness invites consideration without demanding agreement, recognizing each person as **sovereign of their own mind**, free to decide what to embrace, question, or leave behind.
