### Discernment — Candidate Fiat Anchored Ambiguity

**Discernment — At least this: the capacity and activity of distinguishing what is encountered from what is inferred, imagined, preferred, or justified, so that the meaning, possible sources, and consequences of an encounter may be responsibly understood and engaged. But perhaps more?**

Within Transcendent Openness, Discernment may concern recognizing That Which Is Larger through its Shadow.

It engages perception, bodily feeling, intuition, imagination, reason, testimony, and further inquiry without requiring that any one source provide infallible access to what remains beyond present understanding.

Discernment may identify a strongly warranted explanation, preserve several plausible interpretations, reject unsupported possibilities, or recognize that no available explanation is yet adequate.

It can also support decisions about whether and how a relationship should be cultivated, even when its ultimate nature remains unresolved.

Discernment is distinguishable from Analogical Discernment, which examines the scope and justification of analogical applications, and Fruitional Discernment, which examines what follows from possibilities and their realization.

These are related activities whose distinctions may warrant further investigation without requiring separate methods or duplicative definitions.
