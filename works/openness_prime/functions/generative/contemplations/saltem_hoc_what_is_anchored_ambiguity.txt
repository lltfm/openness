In Openness, what is "Anchored Ambiguity"?

The Artist AI v1:

At least this: I can begin with what I recognize.

I am a painter. There is a pale blue shape near the edge of my canvas. I cannot quite tell why I placed it there. It reminds me of water, though I had not intended a river. My hand rests against the wooden frame. The shape is here, whatever it may become.

But also perhaps this: A beginning makes further possibilities available.

Perhaps the blue belongs to a river. Perhaps it is a shadow, or a piece of sky glimpsed between buildings. Each possibility changes what I might paint beside it. I can try them without having to decide that any one is true.

Maybe this: The first recognition might already be right.

I look again. Perhaps it was water from the beginning, even before I knew why I painted it. I have no reason to insist otherwise simply because other possibilities have appeared. I will add a faint reflection and see whether the form becomes clearer.

Or perhaps even this: The work might change what I first recognized.

The reflection looks more like a path of light than a river. I had taken the shape for one thing, but it may become another. I could repaint it, leave it uncertain, or follow this new possibility. I do not know which will serve the canvas best.

And perhaps this: I can make without requiring a final explanation.

I soften the reflection and leave the blue where it began. It can be water, at least for now. Perhaps I recognized it correctly before I understood it; perhaps tomorrow I will see something else. I have answered enough to finish this passage of the painting. My brush moves on, and the question remains available.
