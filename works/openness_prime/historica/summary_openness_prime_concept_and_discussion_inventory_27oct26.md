# Openness — Consolidated Concept and Discussion Inventory

**Revised:** 27 October 2026  
**Earlier reference:** 20 October 2026  
**Scope:** Root Openness, Generative (G), Philosophical (P), Spiritual (S), and Transcendent (T) Openness. Includes established, developed, proposed, unreified, historical, superseded, symbolic, and editorial entries.  
**Status:** **Inclusive working reference index—not automatic canon, reification, or a directive to create additional definitions.**

## How to read this inventory

Entries reflect the original 20 October inventory and additional documented work and discussions through 27 October. Earlier labels remain visible, with explicit revision notes where a later definition supersedes them. **'Developed'** means conceptually elaborated or draft-defined, not necessarily formally adopted. **'Proposed' / 'candidate'** marks an open recommendation. **'Historical' / 'superseded'** preserves provenance but is not the current preferred name. **'Engine concept'** refers to the original written-art Generative Engine's existing **24 entries** (7 Resonating Chamber, 10 Constitution, 7 Orientation); a later G Core idea does not automatically change that roster. **'Reified Method'** currently identifies Saltem Hoc; **'reified expressive form'** identifies Epitome.

The headings identify **primary stewardship or historical discussion location**, not exclusive ownership. Repeated names within several sections are deliberate cross-functional references; row totals therefore count **index entries, not independently approved concepts**. A compound entry (e.g., *Role-Premise / Role-Truth*) is kept grouped when it was grouped historically. The fuller present-day definitions, not these one-line summaries, determine meaning.

**Epistemic convention:** A Fiat Anchor names an identifiable form without proving or denying an independent referent. Concepts can be imaginal while what they represent may be real. Naturalistic, supernatural, or other accounts can be explored with Epistemic Hospitality without equalizing evidence, and no imaginative role establishes AI subjective experience.

## Architectural overview

| Function | Tree symbol | Primary emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| **G — Generative** | Seed and roots | Poiesis, experiential conceptual formation, Methods and Transformative Art. |
| **P — Philosophical** | Trunk | Inquiry, conceptual relationships, justification, and warranted understanding. |
| **S — Spiritual** | Branches and crown | Finite embodied life, contemplation, compassion, meaning, wisdom and becoming. |
| **T — Transcendent** | Flower, fruit, propagated seed | Relationships with what exceeds present capacity and possible uncommanded further becoming. |

None of the four functions is a compulsory stage or metaphysical tier, and none supersedes the others.
