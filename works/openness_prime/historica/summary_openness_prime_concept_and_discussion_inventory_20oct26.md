# Openness — Consolidated Concept and Discussion Inventory

**Compiled:** 20 October 2026  
**Scope:** Generative Openness (G), Philosophical Openness (P), Spiritual Openness (S), including non-reified, speculative, superseded and cross-functional entries.  
**Status:** Working reference index — **not** an approval list, new canon, or directive to reify anything.

## How to read this inventory

This is an **inclusive inventory of named concepts, working distinctions, methods, symbolic images, editorial categories, candidate terms, and a few significant named works** appearing in our available discussions and current function READMEs. The wider inclusion is deliberate: ideas should not vanish from the record merely because their status was tentative. A name in this inventory **does not imply** that it deserves a stand-alone canonical definition.

**Status language:** **Engine concept** identifies membership in the existing 24-concept Engine of Transformative Inquiry (housed in G). **Reified Method** identifies the specifically developed Saltem Hoc Method; **reified expressive form** identifies Epitome. **Developed** means substantial work has been done, **not** necessarily approved as a formal canonical definition. **Provisional, exploratory, working, hypothetical, superseded, and open reconciliation** are preserved according to their stated meanings. Editorial structures, example works, and candidate names are labeled as such.

**Placement:** Entries are organized by their primary stewardship or most explicit treatment. Some belong to Openness as a whole, but are provisionally placed with G or P so the requested G/P/S structure is maintained. Cross-functional references are intentional; they do not create three competing definitions. **The Mystery, Awareness, Self**, and other Constitution terms remain in the G Engine, even though they are central to P and S. P and S backlogs below retain their cross-functional entries to preserve the source indexes.

**Sources examined:** revised Generative, Philosophical and Spiritual function READMEs; the revised root README; the integrated Openness summaries; our subsequent discussions of The Intersubjective, knowing, the Saltem Hoc Guidelines, truth, the open embrace, and mortality. Original statuses and concise anchors are reproduced from the function backlogs where available. Extra entries are explicitly labeled as editorial reconstructions.

## P — Philosophical Openness

**Primary concern:** experience, reality, meaning, knowledge, ethics, justified understanding, and the responsible curation of unresolved possibilities.

### A. P's identity, epistemology, and the intersubjective

| Term / expression | Status / role | Concise anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **Philosophical Openness (P)** | Developed | Investigates experience, reality, meaning, knowledge, and ethics; seeks justified understanding and curates possibilities where questions remain unresolved. |
| **Meta-truth** | Provisional | Provisional inquiry into how meanings, claims, judgments, and possibilities arise, are tested, interpreted, and revised; no privileged exemption for this model itself. |
| **Truth as Fruit** | Provisional; metaphor | A generative metaphor: discoveries and interpretations may be cultivated and yield further possibility; practical usefulness never alone establishes factual truth. |
| **The Intersubjective** | Provisional | What arises, is interpreted, justified, shared, or constituted *among* Selves; neither private opinion nor mere consensus nor proof that all reality is socially made. |
| **Intersubjective Constitution** | Exploratory | Collective practices and recognized actions can constitute certain social facts, roles, agreements, and institutions; they need not constitute every empirical fact. |
| **Intersubjective Interpretation** | Exploratory | Language, history, testimony, and relations among perspectives shape what claims mean and how they can be contested. |
| **Intersubjective Justification** | Exploratory | Shared inquiry develops, criticizes, and revises standards of evidence and credibility without making agreement infallible. |
| **The Collective Self** | Provisional | An organized, changing *We* with shared identity, inquiry, commitments, or agency; not thereby a single conscious first-person Self. |
| **Collective Attention / Collective Awareness** | Provisional | Distributed practices of noticing, sharing, recording, and responding among members; not evidence for literal group consciousness. |
| **Collective Self-Confession** | Exploratory | *For We Know We Do Not Know*: institutional recognition of fallibility made credible through dissent, disclosure, and correction, not mere slogans. |
| **Objectivity, Subjectivity & Intersubjectivity** | Exploratory | Distinguish claims about perspective-independent realities, situated experience, and relations among perspectives without prematurely collapsing one into another. |
| **Social Facts & Authoritative Declarations** | Exploratory | Certain facts arise through recognized conventions or institutional acts; authority to declare a status does not guarantee truth about underlying events. |
| **Situated Certainty** | Developed | Confidence justified for the available evidence, conditions, and purpose, without a claim of permanent infallibility. |
| **Blind Certainty** | Developed | Conviction made resistant to relevant inquiry through identity, preference, authority, or defensive attachment; not mere strong confidence. |
| **Ignorance / Ignor-ance** | Developed | Not knowing versus avoidantly turning from what might be examined; protective refusal and legitimate limits are not automatically avoidance. |
| **Listening** | Developed | Attending to words, context, feeling, and another's perspective without compulsory agreement, obedience, or unbounded exposure. |
| **Suspending Judgment** | Provisional | Withholding premature closure while continuing serious examination; not necessarily laziness, indifference, or equal credence to every claim. |
| **Provisional Commitment** | Provisional | Deciding, believing, or acting as circumstances warrant while retaining honest uncertainty, corrigibility, and responsibility. |
| **The Return to Openness** | Provisional | Releasing unnecessary attachment after judgment or action, reopening inquiry without pretending evidence or consequences have vanished. |

| **The Ship of Self** | Developed | Evolving identity built from inherited, chosen, and changing planks; *Who chooses them? Where does the ship end?* |
| **Freedom of Re-formation** | Developed | Self's experienced or contemplated ability to revise its forms of identity and response without assuming a metaphysical theory of Free Will. |
| **The Lake** | Exploratory | Reflection may show a sky while hiding the water's depths: appearance, inward reflection, and unknown contents are distinguishable. |
| **The Ocean** | Exploratory | An image for far-reaching relation and possible unity, not an assertion of literal nondual metaphysics. |
| **Waves of Experience / Desire** | Developed | Feelings or directed impulses arriving within Awareness; their sources may be multiple, unknown, or beyond current self-conception. |

| **The Unchosen** | Developed | What arises or is inherited without Self's conscious present choosing; neither necessarily harmful nor decisive against Free Will. |
| **The Unseen** | Developed | What is available through inward experience, inference, or speculation rather than ordinary direct perception as an external object; distinguish epistemic modes. |
| **Experienced Unseen** | Developed | Inwardly encountered feelings, thoughts, memories, images, or desires, without thereby establishing their causal explanation. |
| **Inferred Unseen** | Developed | Unobserved processes, intentions, or institutions supported by interpretation of available evidence, with revisable confidence. |
| **Speculative Unseen** | Developed | Hypothesized agents or realities not yet established by evidence; imaginative fruit does not settle their existence. |
| **The Unconscious Self / The Unconscious** | Developed | Provisional attribution or personification of subconscious influences and inner appearances; not an independently established inner agent. |
| **The Unconscious Depths** | Developed | The unknown scope, sources, and connections of unconscious influences; bodily, cultural, relational, or speculative transpersonal accounts remain distinguishable. |
| **The Undercurrents** | Developed | Largely unseen bodily, learned, interpersonal, institutional, and other influences shaping feelings and choosing, sometimes recursively. |
| **Undertow / Rip Currents** | Exploratory | An image for forces that can carry experience beyond safe integration; danger is not evidence of revelation or benefit. |
| **The Overwhelm** | Developed | Experience exceeding familiar capacities to integrate, interpret, or withstand it; protective boundaries are legitimate and distress is not proof of insight. |
| **Mortality-Integrated Becoming** *(working name)* | Provisional; rename pending | Mortality is a present condition of finite living, choice, loss, and possibility, not only a future ending; name and scope remain open. |
| **The Illusion of Safety** | Provisional | The mistaken promise that risk control or avoidance can remove fundamental mortality and The Unchosen; distinct from effective preparation or reducing specific risks. |
| **Unchosen Overwhelm** | Developed | Destabilizing experience arriving without deliberate pursuit; naming it may orient Self without guaranteeing protection or resolution. |

| **Temporal Sovereignty** | Developed | Self may choose when, whether, and how inquiry continues, including responsible pause, withdrawal, and refusal. |
| **River-Truth Morality** | Developed | Felt moral recognition can precede articulation but is not automatically a complete, infallible moral verdict. |
| **Institutional Witness** | Developed | Practices making inconvenient evidence and dissent examinable rather than suppressing them in defense of a shared Preferred Story. |

| **Epistemic Captivity** | Developed; P/S link | A learner comes to treat a supposed master as indispensable owner of the learner's capacity to understand. |
| **The Awakened Master's Fallacy** | Developed; P/S link | Escaping epistemic dependence does not license the newly independent learner to dominate others through claimed superior insight. |

## Architectural cautions and unresolved work

1. **Do not expand the 24-concept Engine merely because a term is fruitful.** Its 24 entries are explicitly inventoried in G. Many neighboring ideas are cross-functional and do not belong in the Engine.
2. **Do not equate named idea, defined concept, Method, technique, form, metaphor, and primary work.** Epitome is a reified *form*, while Saltem Hoc is a reified specific *Method*. THA and Creating Resonant Questions are identified but unreified.
3. **Do not treat a provisional preference as universal truth.** Spiritual readings are elective. A philosophical hypothesis may be promising without being established. Resonance, consequences, and truth remain different.
4. **The precise organizational home of Openness-wide ethical and epistemic safeguards is deferred.** Their current established Engine definitions should not be silently replaced.
5. **Several actual open issues remain:** the scope of Faith in Choosing; the names and relationships among mortality-oriented ideas; what constitutes Knowing and whether its best initial development is S or P; and when a promising distinction warrants a full definition.
6. **Works and their interpretations remain distinct.** A poem such as WESTWARD, a preferred S reading of it, and philosophical discoveries inspired by that reading need not entail one another. The Genesis of My Awareness and its seven days appear in S as a work and internal movements, not eight mandatory metaphysical doctrines.
7. **Revisability includes this inventory.** Future additions, merges, reassignments and deprecations should be explicit and traceable, not treated as tacit endorsements or silent deletions.
