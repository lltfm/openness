# Openness Didactic Document Guidelines

## Purpose and Scope

These guidelines govern **didactic documents throughout Openness**: works whose principal purpose is to introduce, engage, orient, teach, explain, or curate the project's ideas and practices for readers. They include the Openness Prime root `README.md`, the READMEs for **G — Generative**, **P — Philosophical**, **S — Spiritual**, and **T — Transcendent Openness**, as well as conceptual guides, role introductions, practice guides, curated reading paths, and similar reader-facing works.

Their governing aspiration is:

> **Begin with what becomes recognizable and possible. Give the reader a truthful, inviting beginning; unfold further meaning, complexity, and qualification as understanding grows.**

A didactic document makes Openness approachable **without becoming a competing definition**. Its level of detail and literary form may vary. What matters is that readers can recognize an idea, see why it might matter, investigate its grounds, and find the more complete canonical account when they wish.

These guidelines complement the **Openness Definition Guidelines** and the general **Openness Document Guidelines**. Apply their substantive rules according to the document's purpose; do not require a README to have the architecture of a definition, or a technical definition to behave as a beginner's lesson.

## I. Two Kinds of Documents, Two Responsibilities

### 1. Canonical Definitions: The Conceptual Reference

**Definitions establish and safeguard the meaning of a concept in an approved, stewarded version.** The **Foundational Definition** supplies its binding identifying meaning; supporting sections explain, clarify, defend, test, and responsibly situate it. Their priorities are conceptual fidelity, precision, coherence, justification, and adequate coverage.

A definition may be long and technical because the concept needs that depth. Its precise boundaries may appear early where necessary. [**The Mind**](../functions/philosophical/definitions/defining_the_mind.md) is an exemplar of intellectual care, not a length quota; definitions now also use [**Semantic Compression**](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_semantic_compression.md), [**Brevity Without Loss of Essential Meaning**](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_brevity_without_loss_of_essential_meaning.md), and accessible explanatory routes where these improve clarity without obscuring essential meaning.

A definition's version-specific authority is not authority over what independently exists, over a reader's belief, or over a separately stewarded fork. It may be challenged and explicitly revised through the appropriate review process.

### 2. Didactic Documents: The Reader-Facing Encounter

**Didactic documents are interfaces to the conceptual reference works.** They invite readers into ideas, use familiar experiences and explanatory images, connect relevant concepts, and show paths toward further inquiry. Their priorities are recognition, truthful engagement, progressive unfolding, accessible navigation, and respect for the reader's interpretive independence.

They may choose their own order, tone, questions, images, examples, and amount of detail. They may introduce a concept through a concise paraphrase, then link to the maintained definition. **A simplification may omit complexity temporarily, but cannot knowingly assert something that conflicts with the source anchor.** Important qualifications may arrive later only when the opening remains accurate on its own.

| Concern | Canonical definition | Didactic document |
|---|---|---|
| Primary purpose | Establish and safeguard identified meaning | Make that meaning encounterable, teachable, and navigable |
| Principal responsibility | Precision and sufficient coverage | Recognition and progressively useful understanding |
| Length | Whatever conceptual adequacy requires | Whatever the reading path warrants |
| Structure | Definition-specific sections | Flexible order, imagery, examples, and navigation |
| Authority | Maintained, approved meaning within its version | Explanatory and curatorial; subordinate to approved anchors |
| Revision | Requires review of changes to conceptual identity | Editorial freedom without silent redefinition |

The project's *software Bible* analogy expresses the distinction between conceptual reference and approachable interface. It does not make the system infallible or require readers to adopt any of it.

### 3. Mixed-Purpose Documents

A long function README may contain technical detail. A definition may contain a literary Prelude. An introductory guide may quote a Foundational Definition verbatim. **The document's governing purpose, not the presence of a heading, determines its dominant writing standard.**

Preserve exact quotations. When paraphrasing, write for the intended reader but remain faithful to the source. When an attractive simplification actually changes the canonical meaning, flag it as a proposed conceptual revision rather than silently normalizing it.

## II. Governing Principles for Didactic Writing

### 1. Positive Meaning and Affirmative Hospitality First

**Begin by saying what the subject is, does, expresses, or makes possible.** Let the reader encounter an affirmative, intelligible identity before presenting an inventory of exclusions.

An introduction to **Nonimposition**, for example, may begin:

> **Nonimposition** aspires to give the world the greatest possible latitude to be as it is, including our freedom to care for ourselves, establish boundaries, and walk away.

Only later, where useful, explore collisions among freedoms, coercion, consent, necessary intervention, and the distinctive protection against manipulative art.

**Firm opening rule:** The top introductory section must not be organized around definitions by negation, a defensive catalogue of disclaimers, or a list of rejected positions. This does not mechanically ban the grammatical word *not*; *For I Know I Do Not Know* is a meaningful confession, not a negation-led definition.

Distinguish:

- **Definition by negation:** identifying an idea mainly by what it is not.
- **Negation-led framing:** placing exclusions and defenses ahead of an intelligible positive beginning.
- **Contrastive explanation:** illuminating relationships by carefully distinguishing two already recognizable ideas. This remains welcome where useful.

**Affirmative Hospitality Before Negation** extends this principle beyond the opening. When a compatible metaphysical, physical, spiritual, or other interpretation remains unresolved, **welcome it as a possibility for examination** before listing what the anchor fails to establish. Distinguish **not required**, **not established**, and **excluded**. Neither speculative hospitality nor beautiful prose makes an unsupported interpretation true or as credible as a well-grounded account.

This is an editorial orientation, not a rigid three-step Method. Direct exclusions remain appropriate where an interpretation contradicts the concept, misstates evidence, or would license harm.

### 2. The Smallest Truthful Beginning

Offer a recognizable beginning that can genuinely unfold. A useful, optional progression is **recognition → relevance → encounter → further explanation → qualifications → next paths**. The order can change and movements can overlap; neither a numbered teaching sequence nor a prescribed emotional arc is required.

Use natural language before introducing multiple technical terms. One intelligible relationship may be enough to invite further reading. **An opening that is misleading when read alone is not rescued by a disclaimer several pages later.**

Examples of useful beginnings include an open circle as an unfinished embrace, a familiar door that fails to open as expected, the experience of listening inwardly and outwardly, or a tree visited by a bee it cannot see. Each can invite inquiry without pretending the analogy proves everything subsequently contemplated.

### 3. Communicate Possible Fruit Without Promising It

Let readers see why engagement might be worthwhile: more attentive listening, better examination of evidence, recognition of [**Preferred Stories**](../functions/philosophical/definitions/defining_preferred_stories.md), richer imagination, responsible choice under uncertainty, fresh relationships, and opportunities for understanding, creation, living, or becoming.

Separate the **aspiration**, the **invited activity**, the **possible participant experience**, the **observed outcome**, and the **interpretation of its cause**. **Resonance ≠ Fruit ≠ Truth.** A moving metaphor, a fruitful fictional premise, or later growth following adversity does not prove an explanatory story or justify suffering, coercion, or abuse.

The appropriate standard is **affirmative truthfulness**: confidently name possible value while making no unwarranted guarantee.

### 4. Invite Experiential Participation

Connect ideas to ordinary and extraordinary experience: a conversation, a bodily reaction, a dream, an unfamiliar hypothesis, a work of art, a relationship, a hard decision, or the activity of listening. Help readers **notice** a distinction rather than requiring them to memorize terminology before it becomes useful.

[**Resonant Questions**](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_resonant_questions.md), imagery, rhythm, and narrative may perform [**Semantic Compression**](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_semantic_compression.md): an economical expressive form invites a reader to discover connected meanings through participation. A question should be a genuine opening, not an insinuation that dissent reveals ignorance or closedness. A metaphor should not be passed off as literal scientific, historical, or metaphysical evidence.

[**The Marriage of Truth and Beauty**](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_the_marriage_of_truth_and_beauty.md) invites disciplined reasoning and feeling, imagination, intuition, perception, and aesthetic form to inform one another. [**The Faithful Rendering**](../functions/transcendent/core/defining_that_which_is_larger.md#the-faithful-rendering) asks what is being honored, claimed, inferred, imagined, and left open. Let the image reveal a relationship; let discernment examine it.

### 5. Unfold Complexity Where It Matters

Introduce important qualifications when the reader can recognize the question they answer. Place deeper conceptual, epistemic, and architectural distinctions near the relevant encounter, rather than attaching every possible warning to the first sentence.

For instance, a short passage may explain listening as attending inwardly and outwardly. A later passage may ask whether bodily feelings disclose another person's motive or are first evidence of one's own response. Neither early silence on that question nor later complexity should manufacture certainty.

[**Semantic Compression**](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_semantic_compression.md) aims for economy without losing meaningful relationships. [**Brevity Without Loss of Essential Meaning**](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_brevity_without_loss_of_essential_meaning.md) protects the necessary distinctions from being compressed away. Avoid the opposite failure: repetition of identical disclaimers across several sections can make a genuinely open possibility seem quietly excluded. Preserve safeguards where necessary; consolidate where they add no new information.

### 6. Ivory Towers Should Have Flying Taxis

Make difficult work approachable **without diluting what it means**. Give the reader an intelligible entry point, a route to the technically precise account, and a way to return from abstraction to lived examples. Technical terms should reward an interested reader's deeper journey, not punish initial unfamiliarity.

A didactic document may offer a few carefully selected concepts with links to their canonical definitions, rather than presenting the complete conceptual inventory at once. A substantial guide may offer different reading paths for different backgrounds. Accessibility and accuracy reinforce one another when the path actually reaches the concept being taught.

### 7. Preserve Nonimposition and Interpretive Independence

Didactic writing may be evocative, persuasive, emotionally engaging, and confidently committed. **Engagement is welcome; covertly engineered assent is not.** Avoid deceptive selectivity, manufactured urgency, loyalty tests, coercive framing, or insinuations that disagreement proves a reader deficient.

The reader may adopt, reject, question, imaginatively inhabit, decline, adapt, or develop an independent version of an interpretation. [**Perspective-Taking**](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_perspective_taking.md) includes observing, trying on, inhabiting, and stepping back. [**Preferential Inhabitation**](../functions/philosophical/definitions/defining_preferential_inhabitation.md) names a chosen provisional orientation, not a duty to believe.

**A reader's freedom is part of successful communication, not an obstacle to it.**

### 8. Preserve the Distinctive Commitments of Openness

Accessible expression must not silently flatten the ideas that distinguish Openness. Where relevant, preserve the following:

- **Openness** is *the path of becoming ever more open to what is*; it names both a posture and a developing conceptual system.
- [**My Self**](../functions/generative/core/the_seed/definitions/defining_my_self.md), [**My Awareness**](../functions/generative/core/the_seed/definitions/defining_my_awareness.md), and [**My Self-Confession of Not-Knowing**](../functions/generative/core/the_seed/definitions/defining_my_self_confession_of_not_knowing.md) are the three first-person Seed constituents. [**The Self**](../functions/generative/core/definitions/defining_the_self.md) and [**Awareness**](../functions/generative/core/definitions/defining_awareness.md) are distinct generalized concepts. The confession *For I Know I Do Not Know* is its expression, not an interchangeable technical term.
- [**Not-Knowing**](../functions/generative/core/definitions/defining_not_knowing.md) acknowledges incomplete understanding, while [**The Mystery**](../functions/generative/core/definitions/defining_the_mystery.md) concerns what remains unknown or incompletely understood. They are related, not identical.
- [**Suspending Judgment**](../functions/philosophical/core/definitions/defining_suspending_judgment.md) is Openness's standing posture even where evidence makes one account overwhelmingly preferable; it does not cancel discernment or forbid practical decision and action.
- The broad movement of **being open → deciding → acting → returning to openness** is a recognizable orientation, **not a mandatory temporal procedure**. Action may involve acknowledged temporary closedness. The **[Epitome](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_epitome.md) of Openness** likewise invites contemplation, not a compulsory series of steps.
- [**Closedness**](../functions/philosophical/core/definitions/defining_closedness.md), under the current P Core anchor, concerns **the refusal to listen**, including socially organized forms. Lack of attention, fatigue, inability, or temporary focus is not by itself such refusal. [**Blind Certainty**](../functions/philosophical/core/definitions/defining_blind_certainty.md) names **an unawareness of closedness**, not merely high confidence or a forceful tone.
- [**Epistemic Hospitality**](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_epistemic_hospitality.md) welcomes compatible possibilities for inquiry while [**Discernment**](../functions/philosophical/definitions/defining_discernment.md) can strongly favor some explanations and reject others on their evidence.
- **Nonimposition** grants the world latitude, including the freedom to protect oneself and withdraw. Any separate G/P specifications or ethical commitments should be represented in their actual approved context rather than casually fused or treated as competing definitions without review.
- **G, P, S, and T** emphasize Creating, Understanding, Living, and Relating respectively. The Symbolic Tree expresses relationships among them, not a required sequence or hierarchy.
- [**That Which Is Larger**](../functions/transcendent/core/defining_that_which_is_larger.md) is a deliberately chosen **Mental Model**, [**Fiat Anchor**](../functions/generative/core/definitions/defining_fiat_anchor.md), and [**Anchored Ambiguity**](../functions/generative/core/definitions/defining_anchored_ambiguity.md) for considering relationships between a standpoint's grasp and what might exceed it. Its **Shadow**, **Rendering**, and **Faithful Rendering** are named distinctions in one parent definition, not separate entities discovered in the world.

If an existing README disagrees with a subsequently approved anchor, identify the discrepancy for editorial alignment; do not quietly redefine the concept to preserve familiar prose.

### 9. Curate Without Quietly Rewriting

Curation selects what will be useful for this reader. It does not grant new canonical priority to selected concepts or erase unmentioned ones. Distinguish **what the concept means**, **which aspect the guide emphasizes**, **what the reader is invited to explore**, and **where the reader may go next**.

A reader-friendly group of concepts need not itself be a new **Core Concept**, **Generative Method**, or Engine subdivision. **Creating [Resonant Questions](../functions/generative/definitions/defining_resonant_questions.md)** is identified but unreified; its deletion as a separate file does not turn the expressive form **Resonant Questions** into a reified Method. Refer to the current approved inventory and status before making architectural claims.

## III. Guidance by Document Type

### 1. Openness Prime Root README

The root `works/openness_prime/README.md` is a broad first encounter. It should make the posture recognizable, give a meaningful account of the possible fruit of transcendence, show how listening and action relate, introduce the four functions, and offer clear routes to further definitions and works.

An effective possible sequence is: **What is Openness? → The Posture of Openness → Listening and boundaries → The Symbolic Tree → Sample works → Revisability and stewardship → Further commitments**. The current document may use a different sequence or contain other sections; the principle is progressive intelligibility, not compliance with a fixed outline.

The README should open a path into the system, not reproduce the complete technical reference. When it uses an approved concept's canonical name, link it according to the **Openness Document Guidelines**, respecting exclusions for quotations, tables, same-document headings, and Prelude/Coda.

### 2. Function READMEs: G, P, S, T

Each function README should offer a self-contained, positive first encounter with its principal concern and show how the function interacts with the others.

| Function | Principal concern | Reader-facing invitation |
|---|---|---|
| **G — Generative Openness** | Creating | Bring forms and possibilities into recognizable being through creation and inquiry. |
| **P — Philosophical Openness** | Understanding | Examine what is believed or inferred, why, and how concepts relate. |
| **S — Spiritual Openness** | Living | Explore embodied life, attention, love, wisdom, and becoming. |
| **T — Transcendent Openness** | Relating | Cultivate encounters and relationships through which unfamiliar possibilities may arise. |

These are **didactic orienting expressions**, not substitutes for the full functional definitions. A README may next present a short scene, key concepts, relevant roles, cross-functional relationships, and links for further reading. Avoid requiring the reader to master the whole system first.

### 3. Concept Introductions and Teaching Guides

Begin with an experience, a genuine **Resonant Question**, or a succinct affirmative meaning. Develop one or two essential distinctions, illustrate how readers might use them, then link to the precise definition and related works. A guide can be more technical when its readers need that depth; it remains responsible for preserving source meaning.

### 4. Curated Paths, Collections, and Reading Maps

State the guiding question, why the works belong together, what each stop can reveal, and how readers may choose another path. A selection is not an exhaustive inventory or an unannounced new hierarchy. Mark explicitly when a passage is an excerpt, historical formulation, hypothesis, or newly proposed interpretation.

### 5. Roles and Practice Guides

Present what participation may look like before lengthy specification. A role, such as [**The Philomath**](../functions/philosophical/roles/role_the_philomath.md), may be demonstrated through a short exchange before its formal expectations. Separate a role-play orientation from evidence of an AI system's independent inner life or convictions. Do not turn an unreified practice candidate into a guaranteed procedure by presenting it confidently.

## IV. Hyperlinks, Images, and Source Integrity

Follow the general document hyperlinking convention **consistently**, rather than attempting to link every occurrence of a familiar word:

1. Link **capitalized canonical concept references, including unmistakable singular/plural grammatical variants**, to verified source-relative paths. Preserve the form written: [**Mental Model**](../functions/philosophical/definitions/defining_mental_models.md) links to the canonical **Mental Models** definition. Accepted formal synonyms and abbreviations may also point to that home; ordinary lowercase expressions remain unlinked. Do not treat a merely related expression or a distinct formal name as a grammatical variant.
2. Do not link capitalized phrases merely *associated* with a concept: for example, *For I Know I Do Not Know* is not automatically a link to [**My Self-Confession of Not-Knowing**](../functions/generative/core/the_seed/definitions/defining_my_self_confession_of_not_knowing.md).
3. Do not add concept links **inside quoted text, Markdown tables, Prelude or Coda, fenced code, existing links, or image alt text**; do not link headings to sections already in the same document.
4. Link a concept **once in a paragraph or tightly connected passage**; leave an almost immediate repetition unlinked. A later distinct section may link again where useful.
5. Link named internal subterms to their **verified parent-definition heading**, not to invented files. If the internal term lacks a stable heading, flag it for editorial improvement.
6. Verify actual paths from the **source document's own directory**. Openness Prime lives under `works/openness_prime/`; centralized images are at repository-root `images/`. Do not mistake a planned `.md` migration target for a currently working file.

Check spelling, Markdown rendering, image alt text, licensing, exact quotations, and any reused source material. Source links aid navigation; they do not constitute endorsement of a speculative referent.

## V. Craft and Editorial Practice

### 1. Build an Honest Opening

Ask: Can the reader explain what this is? Can they see why it may matter? Does it begin affirmatively? Does any technical name become intelligible before it is required? Does the opening remain truthful if read in isolation? Is its metaphor clearly an invitation rather than disguised evidence?

### 2. Give Depth a Destination

When the document becomes technical, give the reader a reason: a question to resolve, a disputed distinction, a useful practice, a necessary safeguard, or an interesting further possibility. Introduce terms where they perform explanatory work. Remove duplicated caveats; do not remove the one qualification needed to avoid a materially false impression.

### 3. Prefer Examples That Teach

A vivid example should help a reader recognize an actual difference—experienced versus inferred, model versus referent, openness versus refusal to listen, or speculation versus evidence. Use counterexamples when helpful, and label fiction, anecdote, symbolic interpretation, and external evidence appropriately.

### 4. Respect Editorial and Version Authority

The source definition governs its own terms. A popular paraphrase may be revised freely for communication, **but it cannot silently change a Foundational Definition**. When a substantive contradiction appears, document it and propose a separate canonical revision if warranted.

Do not invent or regenerate originator-managed metadata, contributor records, timestamps, version claims, or approval notices. Preserve existing provenance and report unresolved conceptual or link destinations.

## VI. Review Checklist

**Reader-facing quality**

- [ ] The opening makes the subject positively recognizable and is truthful by itself.
- [ ] The text makes relevant possibilities available without long, negation-led defenses or false promises.
- [ ] An experiential example, image, question, or clear natural-language path invites inquiry where helpful.
- [ ] Complexity unfolds in response to a meaningful need; accessible language does not displace precision.
- [ ] Strong claims, speculations, quotations, metaphors, evidence, and reported fruit are distinguishable.
- [ ] The reader retains freedom to question, prefer, decline, and develop another understanding.

**Conceptual and editorial integrity**

- [ ] Approved concept names, anchors, G/P/S/T relationships, statuses, and distinctions are represented accurately.
- [ ] Didactic simplifications have not silently rewritten definitions, including [**Closedness**](../functions/philosophical/core/definitions/defining_closedness.md), [**Blind Certainty**](../functions/philosophical/core/definitions/defining_blind_certainty.md), and [**TWIL**](../functions/transcendent/core/defining_that_which_is_larger.md).
- [ ] Capitalized canonical names **and clear grammatical singular/plural variants** are linked when appropriate; unrelated or merely associated expressions, quoted material, tables, Prelude/Coda, and nearby repetitions remain unlinked.
- [ ] Destinations, heading anchors, image paths, Markdown rendering, and planned `.txt` migrations are checked.
- [ ] Unresolved dual homes and absent definitions are flagged rather than guessed.
- [ ] Authorship, originator approval, and review status have not been invented.

## VII. Governing Standard

**Definitions establish meaning within a maintained version. Didactic documents help readers encounter, explore, and use that meaning.**

Begin with the recognizable. Invite the reader into what may become possible. Unfold what understanding requires. Preserve the distinctions that make inquiry honest—and the freedom to continue beyond the page.
